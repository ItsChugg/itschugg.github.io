Evolutionary

Evolutionary is a fictional universe centered around biology and mostly hard science-fiction. It draws heavily from a multitude of games and stories for inspiration. A recurring theme is that while life is fairly common throughout the universe, it is so vast and diverse that it is largely incompatible with most other forms, breathing life into the technological and societal elements needed for a species to become space-faring.
