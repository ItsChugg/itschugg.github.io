Hurricane Fungus

Scientific Name: Mycelia Rotantia

Summary §

Hurricane Fungus is a type of mycelia that seems to thrive on motion. For whatever reason, they seem to have specifically evolved to have a "symbiotic" relationship with Hurricanes, in which Hurricanes feed the mycelia in times of abundance, such as when it is at the equator and feeding on the temperature differential of the atmosphere. In situations where the hurricane would dissipate and disappear, the mycelia will sacrifice their stored energy and dump it into the hurricane itself, not only strengthening its power, but additionally giving them the ability to steer the hurricane in a direction they desire. This results in hurricanes that are decades, sometimes even centuries old.

Oddly enough, because of the fungus, Hurricanes are given the ability to "reproduce", in which the fungus will dump their energy to forcibly split the hurricane into two, opposite spinning hurricanes. While it has not been seen, it is hypothesized that the fungus may also be capable of merging two opposite hurricanes into one.
