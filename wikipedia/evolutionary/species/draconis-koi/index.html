Draconis Koi

Scientific Name: Dolor Piscium

Summary §

Named for being found on a planet in the Sigma Draconis system, these fish pose a remarkable intelligence. Originally discovered by creeped-out Russian colonists realizing they were being observed by a swarm of them, further examination and testing revealed their capability of to recognize individual researchers, basic symbols, and objects. Additionally, they seem to have a basic form of body language, which seems to be how and how fast they move their mouths. In fact, these motions can be fast enough to produce bubbles, in a similar manner that Pistol Shrimp from Earth can.
