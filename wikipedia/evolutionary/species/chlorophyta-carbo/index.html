Chlorophyta carbo

A form of Algae that lives primarily in clouds or atop the peaks of mountains.

Summary §

Chlorophyta Carbo generally reproduce and divide within the moist clouds of the atmosphere. They remain afloat from splitting water molecules, and keeping the buoyant hydrogen within their cell walls, while releasing the oxygen. Occasionally they'll burst, and fall gently down onto the surface, before eventually floating back up again. They can hamper airspace with their presence.
