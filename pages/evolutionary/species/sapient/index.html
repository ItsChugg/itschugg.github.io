Sapient species in the Evolutionary universe are those that have achieved self-awareness, complex social structures, and — in most cases — some degree of Prion sensitivity. The following documented species represent only a fraction of known sapient life; the full catalogue is ongoing.

Aquins §

"We thought it was a particularly resistant ooze upon bringing it aboard our spacefaring vessel. It began to ripple in a volatile manner, eventually resting as it warmed. The aqueous mass felt like water, albeit having been jellified, but there was more to it: the blob coalesced into an upright form, sprouting tentacles that became appendages, and molded its head to mimic my own. It spoke to us curiously (more than us, despite our befuddlement) after developing vocal cords — for we saw the process with our own eyes! — within its translucent flesh. We were profoundly in awe…"

— Spack Jarrow, An Amorphous Encounter

A pelagic, amoeboid species, aquins are defined by their fluid physiology; juveniles begin as amorphous blobs, resembling humanoid forms as they mature. They disperse their cognitive faculties throughout their bodies for maximal survivability, but are prone to risk permanent mental disabilities. An aquin may also adjust the acidity and basicity of its body to dissolve and consume various nutrients. If enough matter is acquired, unique progenitor cells can be produced to "mimic" those of different organisms (chloroplasts, myocytes, etc.).

Caelcoels §

The sky darkened.
Hundreds swooped low; thousands covered the land in shadow.
Blades of grass took in the breeze.
A symphony rang out; familiar songs were sung aloud.
Then the land grew quiet once more.
It waited for the birds to come again.

— Lok'gma'chk, Songs of the Wind

Genetically similar to raptors and songbirds, caelcoels are a broad, avian species that range from anywhere between a half and whole meter in size. They are regarded for their beaks and the tiny holes they sport; whenever they breathe or move around, a unique noise is played, whereby each caelcoel retains a different "whistling" sound. Their lung capacity and method of camouflage are also exceptional in quality. Caelcoels prefer habitats interspersed with arboreal patches and grassy fringes, allowing them to migrate and hunt for prey at the same time, and they communicate orally through chirps, squawks, and songs.

Livionians §

Droplets braved the great descent, their numbers and speed growing by the minute. Lhea welcomed the sudden downpour; her plant was given a massage, causing it to straighten and absorb the rain for some time. She felt the warmness of the plants that surrounded her, yet it was not to last for long, as the precipitation became too much to bear. Plants wailed. Animals fled. Hundreds of disheartening sensations flooded Lhea's thoughts. The final straw came when her own plant suffered, leading the livionian to shelter the symbiote and retreat to a nearby cave opening. Distant cries echoed throughout her mind, chambered by the abandoned hillside cavity…

— M. D. Wellington, Symbiosis

Livionians are a herbivorous species that have a symbiotic mutualism with a particular plant species. They were once preyed upon by carnivores, developing acute sensory systems and streamlined bodies to detect and avoid predators; a livionian's ears can respond to infra- and ultrasound, whereas its tail supports arboreal locomotion. The pheromones of their plant symbiotes allow them to predict local weather patterns, assess the health of nearby organic matter, and partially sustain themselves by photosynthesizing sunlight. Livionians' fur coats are also colorful and have been known to be individually styled.
