Evolutionary

Overview

Evolutionary is a fictional universe developed by Russell G. Rinaldo and associates, centered around the concepts of sci-fi, magic, evolution, and biology. It is constantly under development and is subject to change.

Summary

Inspired by games and concepts such as Stellaris, Spore, and Dune, Evolutionary blends hard science fiction with arcane biology. Civilizations rise and fall across the cosmos, shaped by the peculiar properties of Prion, the forces of elemental magic, and the relentless pressure of evolution.
